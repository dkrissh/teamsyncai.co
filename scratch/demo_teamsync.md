**Last updated:** 2026-04-30

---

## What the page does

Visitors land here from email, ads, or direct traffic. The page:

1. **States the problem** — "The hardest part of hiring isn't finding candidates — it's knowing which one is right for your team."
2. **Explains the output** — TeamSyncAI generates a complete Hiring Blueprint from a few questions about the role: evaluation goals, interview questions, failure signals, 30/60/90-day indicators, and post-hire check-ins.
3. **Shows a sample** — A real example Blueprint generated from a two-line Operations Manager description. Visitors can download the PDF free or sign up to generate their own.
4. **Captures the lead** — Name + work email form with Cloudflare Turnstile captcha and Terms/Privacy consent. On submit, redirects to the app where they build their blueprint.
